## 📖 Overview

This repository contains the course materials, slide decks, and lab resources for **ECUE 3.1.3: Neural Communication** taught at CESI (Bachelor AI, Year 2).

The course establishes the formal bridge connecting biological electrical/chemical communication principles to formal mathematical abstractions and artificial computational architectures.

### What This Module IS and IS NOT

* **What It IS:**
  * **The Formal Bridge:** Connecting biological electrical/chemical communication principles to formal mathematical abstractions.
  * **First-Principles AI:** Understanding *why* weights, thresholds, activations, and architectures are designed the way they are.
  * **Scratch Implementation:** Building the core computational blocks in pure Python/NumPy.
* **What It is NOT:**
  * **Not a pure Neuroscience course:** No memorization of hundreds of anatomical regions, molecular pathways, or clinical pathologies.
  * **Not a black-box Deep Learning course:** Not simply calling `import tensorflow` or `torch.nn` to train massive models without inspecting internal mechanics.

### Course Roadmap

* **Part 1: Foundations**
  * **Introduction & Epistemological Bridge**
    * *Scope and core concepts*
  * **The Biological Neuron**
    * *Conceptual challenge: What is a neuron?*
  * **From Biology to Mathematics**
    * *Abstraction and Information encoding*
  * **Logical Gates & McCulloch-Pitts Neurons**
    * *Live coding: simulation on NumPy*

* **Part 2: Architectures & Learning**
  * **The First Learning Neuron**
    * *From static M&P model to plasticity*
  * **Limits & Evolution**
    * *The XOR Crisis*
  * **Multi Layer Perceptrons**
    * *Forward propagation and communication*
  * **Synthesis & Closure**
    * *Closing the Epistemological Bridge*
